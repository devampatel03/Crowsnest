# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Crowsnest to be a friendly, harassment-free place to work on for everyone, whatever their
experience, background, identity or ability.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful, and accept it the same way.
- Respect that people work on this in their own time.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Anything else that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the
[Security tab](https://github.com/devampatel03/Crowsnest/security) and say it is a conduct report, or
contact the maintainer, [@devampatel03](https://github.com/devampatel03), through GitHub. Reports are
handled privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these
expectations.
